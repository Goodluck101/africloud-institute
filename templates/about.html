About AfriCloud Institute

AfriCloud Institute is an NGO offering full scholarships to African talent who want to launch and grow a career in tech. We raise communities through live mentoring in Cybersecurity, Cloud, DevOps, AI, Data, Software Engineering, and related skills — so young people become job-ready professionals and entrepreneurs.

CORE VALUES

Excellence

We hold a high bar for skills, teaching quality, and the work students produce.

Adaptability

Curriculum stays aligned with current industry tools and hiring needs.

Community

We raise African talent together — mentorship, peer support, and a scholar community that lasts beyond class.

Innovation

Students learn current platforms, practices, and ways of working used in real teams.

Integrity

Scholarships are free. We are transparent about programmes and how we handle scholar information.
