About AfriCloud Institute

AfriCloud Institute is an instructor-led, career-oriented training platform for working professionals, career changers, and organisations. We teach Cybersecurity, DevOps, Machine Learning, Data Analytics, Software Engineering, and related digital skills.

CORE VALUES

Excellence

We hold a high bar for skills, teaching quality, and the work students produce.

Adaptability

Curriculum stays aligned with current industry tools and hiring needs.

Community

Mentorship, peer support, and the cohort WhatsApp group extend learning beyond class time.

Innovation

Students learn current platforms, practices, and ways of working used in real teams.

Integrity

We are transparent about programmes, fees, and how we handle student information.
